When to go

What is the best time to visit Kenya?

June to October is the dry season and the usual window for the Mara River crossings. January to March is hot, drier, and quieter, with clear Kilimanjaro mornings. April and May are the long rains: some camps close, the parks are green, and prices drop. The month-by-month page covers Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda as well.

Is the Mara River crossing guaranteed?

No. Crossings usually fall between July and October and they happen when the herds decide. We plan those months if a crossing is the reason for the trip, and we say clearly that a particular morning cannot be promised.

Your trip

Are the safaris private?

Yes. The itineraries on this site are private unless a page says otherwise. You get your own vehicle and guide. Group departures are arranged when several parties want the same month. We do not publish dates we cannot run.

Which safari is best for a first visit?

For Kenya, Amboseli plus the Mara, with Nakuru if you have five or more days. For Tanzania, Tarangire, the Serengeti and Ngorongoro. Add gorillas only if you have the extra days and the permit budget. A first trip that tries to do all four countries is usually too much driving.

Can I add a beach?

Yes. Diani, Watamu and Mombasa are the Kenya coast. Zanzibar is the usual add-on after a Tanzania safari, and it also works after a Kenya trip if you fly. Plan three or four nights. One night is an airport exercise.

Before you pay

Do I need a visa?

Most visitors to Kenya apply for an electronic travel authorisation before flying, on the official government site. Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda have their own eVisa or visa-on-arrival rules, and an East Africa tourist visa can cover Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda for some passports. Rules change. We send the current official link for your nationality before you pay a deposit, and we do not guess on the form.

How much does a safari cost?

A private mid-range Kenya safari is often in the range shown on each trip, per person sharing. Two people in a private vehicle cost more per person than four. Luxury camps and gorilla permits move the figure sharply. The costs article explains what is inside the number. The quote we email is the price.

How do I pay, and what if I cancel?

A 30 percent deposit confirms the trip. The balance is due 60 days before arrival. Cancellation terms, and the stricter rules on gorilla permits, are on the booking policy page and are repeated on your invoice.
