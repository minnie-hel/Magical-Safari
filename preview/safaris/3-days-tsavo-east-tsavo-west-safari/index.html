3 Days Tsavo East Tsavo West Safari

Package overview

Embark on a thrilling 3-day wildlife adventure through two of Kenya’s most iconic national parks— . Experience the rugged wilderness, breathtaking landscapes, rich wildlife populations, and legendary safari experiences that make Tsavo one of the largest and most diverse ecosystems in East Africa. Enjoy game drives, visit historical sites, and witness spectacular natural attractions such as

  • Tsavo East National Park
  • "Red" Elephants: The elephants here are famous for wallowing and dust-bathing in the rich red soil, giving them a distinctive crimson color.
  • Vast Open Wilderness: Experience the sheer scale of one of Kenya's oldest and largest parks, with its beautiful acacia woodlands and sweeping savannah.
  • Tsavo West National Park
  • Mzima Springs: Visit this magical oasis where crystal-clear water gushes from the ground. You can walk to an underwater viewing chamber to see hippos, fish, and crocodiles.
  • Volcanic Scenery: Explore the dramatic terrain, including the Shetani Lava Flows, a dark, solidified river of volcanic rock, offering a stark contrast to the plains.

Day-by-day itinerary

01Day 1 | Mombasa – Tsavo East National Park

Early morning pick-up from your hotel or airport. Drive along the Mombasa–Nairobi Highway, entering Tsavo East through the Bachuma Gate . Begin your first game drive in search of elephants, lions, buffaloes, zebras, giraffes, cheetahs, and the famous red-coated elephants of Tsavo . Arrive at your lodge/camp for check-in and lunch. Afternoon game drive across the park’s vast plains and seasonal watering points. Return to your lodge for dinner and overnight. Sentrim Tsavo Camp Voi Safari Lodge Ashnil Aruba Lodge Meals: Lunch & Dinner.

02Day 2 | Tsavo East - Tsavo West

Early morning game drive as wildlife is most active at dawn. After breakfast, exit Tsavo East and drive to Tsavo West National Park . Strategic stop for game viewing en-route—look out for black rhinos, hartebeests, elephants, and elusive predators. Check in at your lodge/camp and enjoy lunch. Afternoon visit to the iconic Mzima Springs , a crystal-clear oasis fed by underground streams from Mt. Kilimanjaro. View hippos, crocodiles, fish, and birds from the underwater observation chamber. Proceed with a sunset game drive as you return to your lodge. Ngulia Safari Lodge Kilaguni Serena Safari Lodge Voyager Ziwani Camp Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner.

03Day 3 | Tsavo West – Mombasa

Early morning game drive to explore more of Tsavo West’s volcanic landscapes, including the Shetani Lava Flow and scenic viewpoints. Return to your lodge for breakfast. Check out and enjoy an en-route game drive as you exit the park. Drive back to Mombasa, arriving in the afternoon. Drop-off at your hotel or airport — End of Safari . Please note that this safari is fully customizable to suit your travel preferences. You may choose to begin and end the tour in Nairobi, or start in Nairobi and finish in Mombasa if you plan to enjoy a beach extension along the Kenyan Coast. Our experienced Tour Consultants at Magical Africa Safaris are always ready to guide you and help tailor your trip for the best possible wildlife holiday experience. Meals: Breakfast & Lunch.
