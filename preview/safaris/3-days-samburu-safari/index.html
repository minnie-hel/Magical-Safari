3 Days Samburu Safari

Package overview

, a unique semi-arid wilderness known for its dramatic landscapes, rich cultural heritage, and rare wildlife species only found in Northern Kenya. Samburu is home to the special “ ” — the Grevy’s zebra, Beisa oryx, reticulated giraffe, gerenuk, and Somali ostrich — along with elephants, lions, leopards, and abundant birdlife. Enjoy scenic game drives along the Ewaso Nyiro River, which attracts wildlife throughout the year.

  • Samburu National Reserve
  • Encounter rare northern species in Samburu, including Grevy’s zebra, Somali ostrich, and Beisa oryx
  • Predator Action: High chances of seeing lions, leopards, and cheetahs, often with dramatic kills, especially near the river
  • Elephant Herds: Large elephant populations gather at the Ewaso Ng'iro River, offering great viewing and bathing spectacles.
  • Birdwatching Paradise: Over 450 bird species, including the Vulturine Guineafowl and Martial Eagle, make it a top birding destination in Kenya.

Day-by-day itinerary

01Day 1 | Nairobi – Samburu National Reserve

Morning Depart Nairobi after breakfast and drive north through Nanyuki with stunning views of Mt. Kenya. Arrive in Samburu in time for lunch at your lodge/camp. Afternoon Enjoy your first game drive in the reserve. Look out for the Samburu Five and large herds of elephants near the Ewaso Nyiro River. Evening Return to the lodge for dinner and overnight.

02Day 2 | Full Day in Samburu

Morning Early morning game drive when wildlife is most active. Explore the rugged terrain, riverbanks, and open plains in search of lions, cheetahs, leopards, and the rare species unique to this region. Midday Relax at the lodge/camp; enjoy the pool or panoramic views of the reserve. Afternoon Another exciting game drive with opportunities to spot more wildlife and birds such as the vulturine guineafowl. Evening Dinner and overnight at the lodge.

03Day 3 | Samburu – Nairobi

Morning Sunrise game drive before returning to the lodge for breakfast. Check out and depart Samburu. Afternoon Stop for lunch en route. Arrive back in Nairobi in the late afternoon/evening, marking the end of your wilderness adventure. Please note that this safari is fully customizable to suit your travel preferences. You may choose to begin and end the tour in Nairobi, or start in Nairobi and finish in Mombasa if you plan to enjoy a beach extension along the Kenyan Coast. Our experienced Tour Consultants at Magical Africa Safaris are always ready to guide you and help tailor your trip for the best possible wildlife holiday experience.
