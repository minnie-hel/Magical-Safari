1 Day Shimba Hills Tour

Package overview

, one of Kenya’s most scenic coastal reserves known for its lush forests, rolling hills, rich wildlife, and the iconic —a species found nowhere else in Kenya. This tour combines wildlife viewing, forest walks, breathtaking viewpoints, and a visit to the majestic

  • Shimba Hills National Reserve
  • Sheldrick Falls: A major attraction, reached via a guided 2-hour walk through dense forest, leading to a refreshing natural pool at the base of the 25m waterfall.
  • Sable Antelope: Home to Kenya's largest population of these rare, beautiful antelope.
  • Wildlife: See forest elephants, giraffes, buffalo, monkeys (Blue, Sykes), and elusive leopards, plus over 200 bird species.
  • Coastal Rainforest: Experience a unique ecosystem with cool, misty air and lush vegetation, a contrast to the nearby beaches.
  • Viewpoints: Enjoy panoramic views of the coastal plains and the Indian Ocean from elevated spots like Elephant Hill.

This one starts from the coast, not from Nairobi.

Day-by-day itinerary

01Day 1 | Mombasa/Diani – Shimba Hills - Mombasa/Diani

Pick-Up & Departure 06:00 AM – 06:30 AM: Pick up from your hotel or residence in Mombasa or Diani . Depart for Shimba Hills National Reserve, arriving at the gate by mid-morning. Morning Game Drive 08:00 AM – 10:00 AM Begin your day with a refreshing game drive through Shimba Hills, exploring forested areas and open grasslands. Expect to spot: Sable Antelopes Elephants Giraffes Buffalo Warthogs Waterbucks Various bird species The cool climate and diverse landscapes make for a relaxed and scenic game drive. Guided Nature Walk to Sheldrick Falls 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM Embark on a guided walk through the forest to the spectacular Sheldrick Falls . The hike (about 1.5–2 hours round trip) offers: Beautiful forest trails Birdlife and butterflies A refreshing natural pool at the bottom of the waterfall You may take photos or enjoy a cool dip (optional). Lunch at Shimba Hills Lodge 12:30 PM – 2:00 PM Enjoy a delicious lunch at Shimba Hills Lodge , famous for its serene forest setting and waterhole that attracts wildlife. Relax on the terrace and watch animals come to drink. Afternoon Game Viewing 02:00 PM – 03:30 PM Continue with a short afternoon game drive as you begin your exit from the reserve. Drive Back 03:30 PM – 05:30 PM Depart Shimba Hills and drive back to your hotel/residence in Mombasa or Diani. Drop-off in the early evening.
