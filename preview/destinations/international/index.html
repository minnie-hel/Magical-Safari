Destinations

International destinations

Beyond Kenya: the Serengeti, Ngorongoro and Zanzibar; Bwindi, Mgahinga and Queen Elizabeth; Volcanoes, Akagera, Nyungwe and Lake Kivu.

Tanzania

Serengeti

The Serengeti is where the herds spend most of the year. Southern plains for the calving in January and February, the western corridor around June, and the north toward the Mara River from July. A good Tanzania plan follows that calendar instead of using the same lodge every month.

Depends on what you want to see. The month-by-month guide is the useful page. Two or three nights.

Ngorongoro

Most visitors spend a morning on the crater floor. It is a small, busy, very productive piece of habitat: lion, hyena, and often rhino. You do not need two descents. A night on the rim is the comfortable way to do the early start.

Year-round. April and May are quieter and wetter. One night on the rim, or a day visit from Karatu.

Zanzibar

Zanzibar works as three or four nights at the end of a Kenya or Tanzania safari. Stone Town is a half day. The north and east coasts are the beach. We book the hotel and the flights. We do not pretend to run the island.

June to October, and December to February. The long rains in April and May are the wettest. Three or four nights.

Uganda

Bwindi

Bwindi holds a large share of the world’s mountain gorillas. You trek with rangers to one habituated family and spend one hour with them. The walk can be short or most of the day, and it can be steep and muddy. A porter is worth hiring, and the fee goes to a local worker.

June to August and December to February are drier. Treks run year-round. Two nights, so the trek day is not also a travel day.

Queen Elizabeth

Queen Elizabeth gives a Uganda trip the classic game drive and a boat among hippos and buffalo on the Kazinga Channel. It pairs with Bwindi. Doing both in a rush makes a poor trip, which is why our shortest version is five days and prefers a flight from Entebbe.

June to September, and December to February. Two nights if the boat and a drive both matter.

Mgahinga

Mgahinga Gorilla National Park is Uganda’s other mountain-gorilla park, on the border with Rwanda and Congo. Groups are smaller than Bwindi’s and permits are limited. Golden monkey tracking is the other walk here. We only put it on a plan when a permit is actually available.

June to August and December to February are drier. Two nights if this is the trek park.

Rwanda

Volcanoes National Park

Volcanoes National Park is the compact version of a gorilla trip. Musanze is about two and a half hours from Kigali. The permit costs more than Uganda’s, the roads are easier, and the trek is still a real mountain walk. Golden monkeys can be added on another day.

June to September, and December to February. Two nights near the park.

Akagera

Akagera is the classic game-drive park in Rwanda: lion, elephant, buffalo, giraffe, zebra and plains game, plus rhino in the fenced sanctuary. It sits in the east, a few hours from Kigali, and is the savannah chapter if a Rwanda trip should not be only gorillas.

June to September, and December to February. Two nights.

Nyungwe

Nyungwe Forest National Park is in the southwest. Chimpanzee tracking and the canopy walk are the two activities most guests book. It is a long way from Volcanoes, so it needs its own nights rather than a day bolted onto a gorilla trek.

June to September, and December to February. Two nights.

Lake Kivu

Lake Kivu is the pause in a Rwanda trip. Boats, a swim and a night in Kibuye, Gisenyi or Karongi. It is not a wildlife park. It works between Nyungwe and Kigali when the driving would otherwise be one long day.

Year-round. One night.
