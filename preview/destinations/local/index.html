Destinations

Local destinations

Kenya is the local circuit: the Mara, Amboseli, both Tsavos, Samburu, Nakuru, Ol Pejeta, Naivasha, Hell’s Gate, Nairobi National Park, Meru, Mount Kenya, Shimba Hills and the Aberdares.

Masai Mara

The Mara is open grassland on the northern side of the Serengeti ecosystem. River crossings, when they happen, are usually between July and October. They are never promised on a particular morning. Outside those months the reserve is still one of the best places in Kenya to look for lion, leopard and cheetah, with fewer vehicles.

July to October for the migration. January to March for fewer vehicles and good cats. Two or three nights. One night is only a glimpse.

Amboseli

Amboseli is a small park with a large elephant population and a clear view of Kilimanjaro when the cloud lifts. Dawn is the honest time to hope for the mountain. The dry months pull elephants onto the swamps, which is why photographs from here are famous.

June to October, and January to February, for drier ground and clearer views. Two nights.

Tsavo East and West

Tsavo East and Tsavo West together cover a huge area, about 22,000 square kilometres if you count both sides. The east is red dust and long views. The west is volcanic, with Mzima Springs and the Salt Lick waterhole. You will not see animals around every corner. That space is the point.

June to October, and again in January and February. Two or three nights if both sides are on the plan.

Lake Nakuru

Nakuru is a fenced park, which makes rhino sightings more reliable than in open reserves. Flamingo numbers rise and fall with the lake. Some years the shore is pink. Some years the birds are on other Rift lakes. We will tell you what the lake is doing in the month you want to travel.

Year-round for rhinos. Flamingo numbers depend on the water. One night on the way to the Mara is enough for most trips.

Samburu

Samburu sits on the Ewaso Nyiro River. The animals people come for are the reticulated giraffe, Grevy’s zebra, gerenuk, Somali ostrich and Beisa oryx. It is hotter and drier than the Mara, and the drives feel different.

June to October, and January to March. The river is the focus in the dry months. Two nights.

Ol Pejeta

Ol Pejeta is private conservancy land on the equator. It is one of the more reliable places in Kenya to see both black and southern white rhino. The last northern white rhinos live here under close protection. That visit is a conservation story, not a wild game drive, and it is not guaranteed as a sighting on every itinerary.

Year-round. The conservancy is fenced and well managed. One or two nights.

Lake Naivasha

Lake Naivasha is a freshwater lake on the floor of the Rift. A boat looks for hippos and waterbirds. Crescent Island is a walking sanctuary on the lake. It pairs with Hell’s Gate on the same day when you already have a night here.

Year-round. Mornings are calmer on the water. One night on a longer Kenya route, or a day visit.

Hell’s Gate

Hell’s Gate sits beside Lake Naivasha. The gorge, Fischer’s Tower and the steam vents are the landmarks. Walking and cycling are allowed on the main tracks, which is unusual in Kenya. It is a day from Naivasha, not a Big Five park.

Year-round. The gorge is hot in the middle of the day. A day visit from Lake Naivasha.

Nairobi National Park

Nairobi National Park is a fenced savannah against the city skyline. Rhino, lion and plains game are realistic on a morning drive. It does not replace a night in the Mara or Amboseli. It is the right use of a spare half day.

Early morning, year-round. Half a day. No need to sleep here.

Meru National Park

Meru is Elsa the lioness country, east of Mount Kenya. Rivers and swamps break up the bush. It is a long drive from Nairobi, so it belongs on a northern circuit with Samburu rather than as a day trip.

June to October, and January to March. Two nights.

Mount Kenya

Mount Kenya National Park is forest and high moorland around the country’s highest peak. Most safari guests use a lodge on the slopes for a night between Ol Pejeta and the northern parks, not a summit climb. Ask if you want a guided walk in the forest.

January to March, and June to October, for clearer views. One night unless you are trekking.

Shimba Hills

Shimba Hills is a forest reserve inland from Diani. Sable antelope are the animal people come to see, with elephant in the forest clearings. It starts from the coast. It is not a day trip from Nairobi.

Year-round. The forest is cooler than the beach. A day from Diani, or one night.

Aberdare

The Aberdare range is montane forest between the Rift and Mount Kenya. The classic stay is a lodge built over a waterhole, where elephant, buffalo and the occasional leopard come after dark. Day game drives are forest tracks, not open plains.

January to March, and June to September. One night.
