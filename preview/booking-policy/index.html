Booking policy

These are our standard terms. Your invoice repeats the ones that apply to your trip. Supplier rules for a particular camp or a gorilla permit can be stricter, and those rules are written on the invoice before you pay.

Deposit and balance

A deposit of 30 percent of the quoted price confirms the booking. The balance is due 60 days before arrival. If you book inside 60 days, the full amount is due when you confirm.

How to pay

We invoice in US dollars and accept international bank transfer. If a card payment is available for your booking, the invoice will say so. Account details appear on the invoice and are confirmed by phone if you ask. We will not ask you to switch to a new account by WhatsApp alone. If that message arrives, call +254 722 326 845 before you send money.

Cancellation

  • More than 60 days before arrival: the deposit is retained. If suppliers release the money, we can move the trip to a new date within 12 months.
  • 59 to 31 days before arrival: 50 percent of the trip cost.
  • 30 days or fewer: 100 percent of the trip cost.

Gorilla permits, once issued, are often non-refundable at the park. That cost is lost even if the rest of the trip is moved. Travel insurance that covers cancellation is your responsibility, and we recommend you buy it when you pay the deposit.

Changes

You can ask for a change of date or lodge. We will tell you the supplier’s fee before we make the change. We may also change a lodge if a camp closes a season. You get the alternative in writing, at the same level, or a refund of the difference.

What the price assumes

Quotes assume two people sharing a room unless they say otherwise. A single room, extra nights, flights and permits are listed as their own lines. Park fees are included for the parks named on the itinerary.
